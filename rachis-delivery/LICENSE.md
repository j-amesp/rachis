## A note on why this licence

RACHIS argues that the federated exchange layer should be public infrastructure —
a published contract that anyone can implement, verify, fork, and take to their own
accreditation, rather than a proprietary artefact that locks a customer to one supplier.

A permissive licence is the licence that matches that argument. Anyone may build on this,
including commercially, including in a competing product. That is the intended outcome:
suppliers competing *above* the federation layer rather than *being* it.

fitness for handling classified or otherwise regulated information, or a representation
that any particular deployment is safe. See `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`.
